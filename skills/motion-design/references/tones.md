# Tone presets

Presets set pacing, type personality, and transition default. Freeform direction overrides voice and subject treatment.

## default

- Energy: playful, clean, postable
- Voice: first person plural, warm, direct, no corporate
- Type: mixed case, comfortable weight
- Scenes: 4–5, each 3–5s
- Hook: a simple question or observation
- Transitions: dip or clean slide
- Use: most product launches that have a personality

## polished

- Energy: serious, elegant, restraint is the idea
- Voice: third person or none
- Type: mixed case, light-to-medium, open tracking
- Scenes: 3–4, each 4–6s
- Hook: one strong image or the name at full scale
- Transitions: slow dip 0.6–0.8s
- Use: premium, infrastructure, "clean", "elegant"

## yc-parody

- Energy: deadpan startup launch, played straight
- Voice: matter-of-fact; the product supplies the joke
- Type: sentence case, heavy or medium, courier for numbers
- Scenes: 4–5, one claim each
- Hook: the problem, stated seriously
- Transitions: hard cut or 0.2s dip
- Use: recursive or absurd concepts that get funnier when treated as Series A

## chaotic

- Energy: fast, loud, unhinged
- Voice: short words, caps allowed, confidence louder than bangs
- Type: heavy, slightly oversized, some scale jumps
- Scenes: 6–8, some under 2s, none over 4s
- Hook: the thing that should not exist, at full volume
- Transitions: hard / flash / zoom cut
- Use: hype, logistics parody, speed

## deadpan

- Energy: calm, dry, nothing registers as unusual
- Voice: one observation, then the product
- Type: large, sparse, one thought at a time
- Scenes: 3–4, holds 4–7s
- Hook: a quiet observation, no setup
- Transitions: 0.8–1.0s dip or a long still
- Use: quote-forward products, dry tools, restraint-as-punchline

## cinematic

- Energy: trailer-scale
- Voice: short declarative sentences that land before the next
- Type: large, full-bleed, caps or heavy mixed case
- Scenes: 4–5, 3–5s, wide
- Hook: a sweeping world claim
- Transitions: wipe or scale-in from 0.95
- Use: nature, scale, grand claims, founder myth

## app-store

- Energy: clean, feature-forward, the product is real
- Voice: feature-benefit, third person, present tense
- Type: title case, medium, no aggression
- Scenes: 4–6, name then 1–2 details
- Hook: name + tagline
- Transitions: 0.35–0.45s slide
- Use: B2B, wellness-adjacent, anything that needs to feel shippable
